Yuting Wu 吴雨庭

PhD Student · Nanyang Technological University

I am a PhD student in the College of Computing and Data Science at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. My research brings together machine learning and control to make autonomous driving systems more robust in the real world.

I work on adversarially robust perception, resilient vehicle control, and adaptive world models. I am particularly interested in how autonomous systems can anticipate and adapt to changing vehicle dynamics, environmental uncertainty, and malicious perturbations.

Before my PhD, I worked as a Research Engineer and Research Associate at NTU. I received my master's degree in Computer Control and Automation from NTU and my bachelor's degree in Automation from Northeastern University, China.

I served as Web Chair for the Green Edge Workshop 2023, held at NTU, Singapore.

Research

From perception to action
01 / WORLD MODELS

Adapting to changing dynamics

I am developing Parallel-JEPA, a parallel world model that separates vehicle dynamics from scene evolution. Current work explores online calibration using prediction residuals, with evaluation across changing physical conditions in CARLA.

World models · Online adaptation · Ongoing
02 / ROBUST PERCEPTION

Dynamic defenses for vision

AdROD uses low-rank HyperNetworks to generate diverse object detectors and couple them with input transformations. My work also investigates physical optical attacks on traffic-sign recognition through the GhostStripe project.

AdROD & related publications ↗
03 / RESILIENT CONTROL

Safer actions under attack

I study learning-based attacks on vehicle actuators and combine reinforcement learning with feedback control for resilient path tracking. This work builds on my earlier research in learning-enhanced PID and PI control.
